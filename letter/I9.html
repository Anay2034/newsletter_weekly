OpenAI Shares New Mathematics Research Results
OpenAI has shared new mathematical results produced by an internal frontier model, including work on open problems. The company published them in a public GitHub repository and formalized many proofs in Lean, a language that lets computers check mathematical proofs.

Key highlights of the release:
  • A Large Body of Work: The repository holds 722 manuscripts in 372 families, from about 4,000 problems the model attempted, with more Lean formalizations still to come.
  • More Detail for Review: OpenAI shared 10 summaries of the model's reasoning and compute estimates, and cautions that some unformalized results may still contain errors.
  • Outside Input: The company consulted an independent advisory group at the Institute for Advanced Study and says it will fund workshops and conferences to examine the work.
The average result used compute equal to about three hours of ChatGPT Pro thinking. OpenAI is working to release the model responsibly but has given no date, and the papers sit on its own GitHub rather than an independent repository.
AI Concept of the Week
Small Language Model: A small language model is an AI model with far fewer internal settings than frontier models, so it runs faster and cheaper on focused, repetitive work like sorting or summarizing. Anthropic's new Claude Haiku 5.5 is one, though smaller models can struggle with harder, open-ended problems.

Clinical Trial Tests Google Chatbot in Urgent Care
At Boston's Beth Israel Deaconess, 98 urgent-care patients chatted with Google's AMIE before seeing doctors, while physicians watched every conversation live. Clinicians said its summaries helped them prepare in 75% of cases and shaped their approach in 57%, though doctors still beat it on practical management plans. Published in The Lancet.

Anthropic Releases Claude Haiku 5.5 at Lower API Prices
Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5 on October 7 at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens for prompts up to 100,000 tokens, with higher rates beyond that. It is built for high-volume work like classification and routing. Anthropic says it costs about 75% less to run than Haiku 4.5, though independent tests show it can use more tokens per task.

IRDAI Leans Against Phasing In Commission Caps
IRDAI member Girija Subramanian said the regulator favours commission caps without a prolonged transition, with January 1 or April 1, 2027 under consideration; expense limits would phase in over five years. Chair Ajay Seth says the proposed Public Insurance Registry could later support blended, outcome-linked pay. All of this remains proposals, with feedback open until October 25.

Care Health Premium Income Rises 44% in September
Care Health Insurance reported September gross direct premium income of ₹1,042 crore, up 44% from ₹723 crore a year earlier, ahead of the roughly 27% industry average it cited. It reports a 3.45% market share and calls itself India's second-largest standalone health insurer. Niva Bupa's premium rose 37.5% to ₹842.8 crore in August.
