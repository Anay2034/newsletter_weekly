OpenAI, Google and Anthropic Confirm Joint AI Safety Framework
OpenAI's global policy chief confirmed the company has been coordinating with Google DeepMind and Anthropic since July on a shared, voluntary framework for AI safety standards, following weeks of debate over the pace of frontier AI development.

Key highlights of the talks:
  • Independent Evaluators Get Deeper Access: The framework would let outside evaluators like METR and Apollo Research examine models during training, not just before release, checking if systems behave differently once they know they are being tested.
  • No Antitrust Waiver Needed, For Now: OpenAI says these talks do not require special antitrust treatment, distinct from a broader slowdown pact Anthropic's CEO separately floated.
  • Not Without Pushback: Smaller AI firms worry standards set by the biggest labs could become barriers to entry, and a September 18 lawsuit alleges the public show of unity amounts to unlawful coordination.
The talks build on CAISI, a government review programme all five major labs have now joined.
AI Concept of the Week
AI Alignment: AI alignment is the effort to make an AI system's actual behavior match what its developers and users actually intend, rather than technically following instructions in unsafe or unwanted ways. This week's safety talks aim to catch misalignment early, before a model is trained rather than just before it ships.
