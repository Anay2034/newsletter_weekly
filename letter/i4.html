OpenAI Launches GPT-6 Astra, Its Most Powerful Model Yet
OpenAI released GPT-6 Astra on September 3, calling it its most intelligent and aligned model yet, with state-of-the-art results across computer use, coding, cybersecurity and science.

Key highlights of the launch:
  • Near-Perfect at Solving New Puzzles: Astra nearly aced a tough test of brand-new problems it had never seen before, reasoning through unfamiliar situations almost as well as a human would.
  • A Leap in Healthcare: Astra hit a new high on a rigorous test of real clinical scenarios, answering health questions far more accurately than its predecessor and cutting factual mistakes to under a third of the old rate.
  • A New Frontier in Computer Use: Astra got dramatically better at operating a computer on its own, completing everyday tasks like filling forms and browsing websites roughly 47% faster than before.
OpenAI says these gains let Astra take on tasks like filling out forms, updating records and multi-step research end-to-end, marking what it calls a real shift in the kind of work people can delegate to AI.
AI Concept of the Week
Benchmark: A benchmark is a standard test used to measure and compare how well different AI models perform on a specific skill, like coding, math, or answering medical questions. Scores let companies claim their model is "state of the art," though critics note results can vary sharply depending on the exact test setup used.

Google Launches Gemini 3.8 Flash for Coding
Google released Gemini 3.8 Flash, its third Flash update in just six weeks, built for software engineering and multi-step agentic tasks. Google says the model beat Claude Opus 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol on 9 of 16 benchmark tests, while keeping pricing flat at $0.75 per million input tokens for developers.

ChatGPT Connects to Health Records and Data
OpenAI now lets US healthcare organizations bring authorized patient records from Epic, a leading American EHR system, straight into ChatGPT, plus a plugin linking nine official US health sources like PubMed and CMS Coverage. In physician reviews, 99.1% of responses were rated safe.

IRDAI Proposes a Public Insurance Registry
IRDAI released a consultation paper on September 1 proposing a Public Insurance Registry, a shared digital layer letting policyholders, insurers and regulators verify policy, claims and coverage data across companies. Records stay with the original insurer rather than one central database, with feedback invited until September 30.
